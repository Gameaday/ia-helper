## Full description (4000 char limit)

IA Helper is your complete companion for the Internet Archive — search, download, and
organize content from archive.org, the world's largest digital library. Whether you're
downloading historical documents, discovering classic media, or building your personal
digital archive, IA Helper makes it effortless.

POWERFUL SEARCH
• Search 35+ million Internet Archive items
• Advanced filters: media type, year and date ranges, collections, languages
• Field-specific queries: title, creator, subject, description, publisher
• Search suggestions and full search history

SMART DOWNLOADS
• Batch download queues with priority scheduling
• Resume interrupted downloads automatically
• Auto-retry with exponential backoff
• Bandwidth throttling — be a good citizen on your own network and theirs
• File rules: include or exclude by format, wildcard patterns, subfolders, and size

OFFLINE LIBRARY
• Organize downloads into collections with tags
• Rich metadata viewer for every item
• Full offline access to everything you've saved
• Smart local storage management

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No accounts.
• Your data never leaves your device
• Open source (MIT license): https://github.com/Gameaday/ia-helper

DESIGNED WITH CARE
• Material Design 3 with full dark mode
• Phone and tablet layouts
• Respects Internet Archive rate limits by design

WHO IT'S FOR
Researchers, genealogists, data hoarders, media collectors — anyone who takes archiving
seriously and wants a serious tool for it.

OUR PROMISE
The core app is free forever. A one-time Pro upgrade for power features is on the roadmap.
We will never charge a subscription, never sell your data, and never show you an ad.

Web version (no install needed): https://gameaday.github.io/ia-helper/app/
Source code: https://github.com/Gameaday/ia-helper
